4. Our lawful basis

We process personal information because it is necessary to perform our contract with you, comply with legal obligations, pursue our legitimate business interests, or where you have provided consent (for example, allowing your vehicle photographs to be used on social media).

5. Sharing your information

We only share information where necessary to operate the business or where required by law. This may include booking software providers, payment providers, website hosting providers, accountants, insurers or legal advisers.

We do not sell customer data.

6. How long we keep your information

We only keep personal information for as long as necessary for business, legal and accounting purposes. When it is no longer required it will be securely deleted or anonymised.

7. Keeping your information secure

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. While no electronic system can guarantee absolute security, we aim to keep your information as secure as reasonably possible.

8. Your rights

Depending on your circumstances, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion.
  • Restrict or object to processing.
  • Request a copy of your data.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
