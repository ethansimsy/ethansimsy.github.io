# Ethan's Auto Care

A simple website for **Ethan's Auto Care**, providing customers with information about services, pricing, terms and conditions, and future online booking.

## About

Ethan's Auto Care is a mobile car cleaning service based in **Scunthorpe, North Lincolnshire**, offering affordable interior and exterior vehicle cleaning at the customer's location.

## Services

### Interior Clean
- Duration: Approximately 1 hour
- From £5
- Includes:
  - Vacuum
  - Interior wipe down
  - Dashboard
  - Doors
  - Windows
  - General interior clean

### Exterior Clean
- Duration: Approximately 2 hours
- From £5
- Includes:
  - Hand wash
  - Wheels
  - Tyres
  - Exterior windows
  - Drying

## Pricing

Prices start from:

| Service | From |
|---------|------|
| Interior Clean | £5 |
| Exterior Clean | £5 |

Final prices are based on the condition of the vehicle and are confirmed before any work begins.
