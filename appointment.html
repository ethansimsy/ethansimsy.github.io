1. Check your booking details

Before the appointment, please make sure the following information is correct:

  • your appointment date and time;
  • the full address where the vehicle will be cleaned;
  • your vehicle registration, make and model;
  • the service you selected; and
  • your telephone number so we can contact you if needed.

If you booked more than one vehicle, please make sure each vehicle has its own appointment and allow enough time between bookings.

2. How to prepare your vehicle

1

Remove valuable and important items

Please remove money, documents, electronics, medication, fragile items and anything sentimental. We cannot accept responsibility for belongings left inside the vehicle.

2

Clear loose rubbish where possible

Small amounts of everyday rubbish are fine, but removing excessive bags, boxes or personal items allows more time to be spent cleaning.

3

Make the vehicle accessible

Park somewhere safe with enough room to move around the vehicle. Avoid narrow roads, restricted parking areas and locations where cleaning is not permitted.

4

Have the keys available

For an interior clean, the vehicle must be unlocked. Please arrange for someone to provide the keys at the appointment time.

5

Tell us about anything unusual

Let us know in advance about heavy pet hair, mould, bodily fluids, sharp objects, pests, severe staining, loose trim, leaks or damaged electrical parts.

6

Keep children and pets away

Cleaning products, cables and equipment may create hazards. Please keep children and pets safely away from the working area.

3. What happens on the day

  1. We will arrive at the agreed address and contact you if necessary.
  2. We will briefly inspect the vehicle and confirm the work required.
  3. The final price will be agreed before cleaning begins.
  4. We may take photographs to record the vehicle's condition.
  5. The vehicle will be cleaned using the selected service.
  6. We will carry out final checks and show you the completed work.
  7. Payment will be due once the service is complete.

Service times are estimates. Heavily soiled vehicles, difficult access and unexpected issues can make an appointment take longer.

5. How pricing works

Advertised prices are starting prices. The final price is based on the vehicle's condition and the amount of work required.

Light condition

Regular dust, light dirt and a generally maintained vehicle.

Moderate condition

More noticeable dirt, rubbish, pet hair, staining or mud that requires extra time.

Heavy condition

Severe soiling, excessive pet hair, embedded dirt, heavy staining or significant rubbish.

Price confirmation

You will always be given a clear price before any work begins.

6. Access, parking, water and electricity

Please make sure the vehicle is parked legally and in a location where there is enough safe working space around it.

Where access to water or electricity has been agreed as part of the appointment, please make sure the supply is available, safe and authorised for use.

If your property has gates, parking restrictions, permits or access codes, please provide the relevant information before the appointment.

7. Weather and outdoor appointments

Exterior cleans may need to be delayed, shortened or rearranged during heavy rain, strong wind, lightning, ice, extreme heat or other unsafe conditions.

Weather changes can happen at short notice.

We will contact you as soon as reasonably possible if the forecast or conditions mean the appointment cannot go ahead safely.

8. Severe vehicle condition and unsafe materials

Please contact us before the appointment if the vehicle contains mould, blood, vomit, faeces, needles, broken glass, chemicals, fuel, pests or any other potentially hazardous material.

We may refuse or stop work if the vehicle is unsafe, presents a health risk, requires specialist equipment or is beyond the service booked.

Complete removal of stains, smells, pet hair and permanent marks cannot be guaranteed.

9. Personal belongings and child seats

Please remove belongings before an interior clean. Items left inside may prevent certain areas from being cleaned.

Child seats will not normally be removed, dismantled or reinstalled. The customer is responsible for ensuring that any child seat is fitted correctly and safely before the vehicle is used.

10. Rearranging or cancelling

Please give as much notice as possible if you need to cancel or move your appointment. Repeated missed appointments or late cancellations may lead to future bookings being declined.

If we need to rearrange because of weather, illness, equipment failure, travel disruption or another unexpected issue, we will contact you and offer another suitable appointment where possible.

Full cancellation information is available in our Terms & Conditions.

11. After the clean

  • Inspect the vehicle before we leave and let us know about any concern.
  • Allow damp interior surfaces or mats to dry fully where applicable.
  • Avoid placing wet or dirty items straight back into the vehicle.
  • Keep your receipt or booking confirmation for your records.
  • Contact us promptly if you notice an issue after the appointment.

Before-and-after photographs may be used for business promotion only with your permission.

12. Frequently asked questions

Do you come to my address?

Yes. Ethan's Auto Care is a mobile service covering Scunthorpe and other nearby locations agreed at booking.

Do I need to be home?

Someone must be available to provide access to the vehicle, confirm the price and make payment, unless another arrangement has been agreed beforehand.

Are the advertised prices fixed?

No. They are starting prices. The final amount depends on the vehicle's condition and is confirmed before work begins.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash is currently accepted. Payment is due once the service is complete.

Can I book two vehicles?

Yes, but each vehicle should have its own booking and enough time must be left between appointments.

What happens if my vehicle is very dirty?

We will inspect it and confirm whether extra time or an increased price is required. You can decline the revised price before any work starts.

Can you guarantee that every stain or smell will be removed?

No. Some stains, odours, scratches and permanent marks cannot be completely removed through a standard clean.

Do you remove pet hair?

Light pet hair may be included, but excessive or embedded pet hair can require extra time and may increase the price.

Will my appointment be cancelled if it rains?

Not always. Light weather may be manageable, but unsafe or unsuitable conditions can mean an exterior appointment needs to be rearranged.

Can I leave belongings in the car?

You should remove valuables, fragile items and loose belongings. Areas blocked by items may not be cleaned.

Do you clean mould, bodily fluids or hazardous waste?

These must be disclosed before booking. We may be unable to accept the work because specialist protective equipment or treatment may be required.

Can you clean a van?

Contact us before booking with the vehicle details and condition. Larger or commercial vehicles may require a longer appointment or different quote.

What should I do if I am unhappy with the result?

Please tell us before we leave where possible. Otherwise, contact us promptly with your appointment details and photographs so the concern can be reviewed.
